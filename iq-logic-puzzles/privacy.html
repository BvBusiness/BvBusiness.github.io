Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

This policy explains what data the IQ Logic Puzzles app ("the app") uses and why. The app is made by BvB.Interactive ("we"), based in the Netherlands. You can reach us at bvb.interactive@gmail.com.

In short
  • There are no accounts. Your puzzle progress is stored on your phone (and in your phone's own backup, if you use it), and we never receive it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads are personalised only if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • We don't sell your data, and the app has no analytics or tracking of its own.

1. Data stored on your device

To track your practice, the app saves the following on your phone:

  • your answers: puzzle type, difficulty, whether you were right, how long you took and which puzzle rules were involved;
  • your XP, levels, streaks, practice days, test results and best times;
  • your settings, such as the chosen difficulty per puzzle.

This data is never sent to us, and we have no servers that could receive it. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android includes it in your phone's backup to your own Google account, so your progress can be restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. Google stores that backup encrypted on your behalf; we can't access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Backup, or Settings → System → Backup).

The data is deleted from your phone when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. A copy in your Google backup is managed by Google under your account's backup settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and funded by banner ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. To show, measure and protect those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects data from your device and sends it to Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID;
  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate your approximate location;
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, operating system, language and app version;
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped one;
  • Diagnostics, such as performance data and errors in showing ads.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, measure how they perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, personalise ads to your interests. Google processes this data as an independent controller under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

We don't receive this data about you individually. AdMob only shows us totals, such as how many ads were shown in a given country.

Your choices about ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the app, Google's consent message asks whether you agree to personalised ads and to the storage and use of information on your device for ads. If you don't agree, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which don't use your past activity. You can change your choice at any time in the app: tap the gear icon on the home screen, then Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, ad personalisation and the storage and use of information on your device for ads rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interest in offering the app for free and keeping its ads safe (Article 6(1)(f)). The data stored on your device in section 1 never reaches us, so we don't process it.

4. International transfers

Google may process ad data outside your country, including in the United States. Google protects such transfers with the safeguards described in its privacy policy, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

5. How long data is kept

Your progress stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its data, and in your Google backup for as long as your backup settings keep it. Google keeps ad data for the periods described in its privacy policy.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and their data was collected for ads, contact us and we will help you reach Google to have it removed.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests about ad data are best sent to Google, but you are welcome to contact us and we will help. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

8. Security

Progress data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. Data sent to Google by the ads SDK travels over encrypted connections.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts using data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in the app.

Common questions

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move to a new phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

How does Auto difficulty work?

Each puzzle type moves up a level after five right answers in a row at your current level (two hint-free solves for Queens), and down after two misses in your last three. You can also pick Easy, Medium or Hard yourself, per puzzle and for mixed practice.

Is this a real IQ test?

No. The app is for practising IQ-style puzzles. Its scores and ratings track your practice and are not an IQ score.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, tap the privacy button at the top of the home screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
